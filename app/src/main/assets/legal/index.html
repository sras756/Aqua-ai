● GOOGLE OAUTH • LEAST-PRIVILEGE AUDIT

Permissions Audit — Google Drive Least-Privilege Scopes

Aqua AI enforces Google's strict least-privilege OAuth model, requesting only the minimum Google Drive scopes needed to store and restore Aqua AI snapshots. Broad Drive scopes (drive, drive.readonly) and all Google Workspace scopes are completely excluded so unrelated files, docs, photos, emails, contacts, and calendars remain invisible to Aqua AI.

ScopeIdentifierAccess Boundary & Least-Privilege Rationale
App Data Folder https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata (drive.appdata) Restricted strictly to Aqua AI's private hidden appDataFolder for aqua_ai_history_backup.json; cannot view personal Drive files.
App-Created Files https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file (drive.file) Per-file access limited to JSON snapshots created or opened by Aqua AI (Aqua_AI_History_*.json) plus Drive /about quota lookup with optional GOOGLE_DRIVE_API_KEY.
Excluded Scopes drive, drive.readonly, Gmail, Calendar, Contacts, READ_MEDIA_* Never requested; keeps unrelated cloud files, media libraries, and Workspace records completely isolated.
