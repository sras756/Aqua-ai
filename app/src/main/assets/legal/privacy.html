1. Information We Access and Process

  • Account Information: Google Sign-In via Firebase Authentication provides your UID, display name, email, and avatar solely to scope your account.
  • AI Prompts & Attachments: Messages, voice transcripts, images, and SAF files you submit are sent to Gemini / Firebase AI Logic when you trigger a request.
  • Live Web Requests: Queries and public URLs fetched when Full Internet Access is enabled carry zero ad trackers.
  • On-Device Session Telemetry: Real battery % and RAM % metrics plotted in Core/Hub remain strictly local to your active session.

2. AI Personas & Custom Directives

Your chosen AI Persona, pinned routines, and custom system directives stay stored locally per user on-device and shape only prompts you send.

3. Permissions, Aqua Launcher & Foreground Service

  • Runtime Permissions: Opt-in RECORD_AUDIO, CAMERA, USE_BIOMETRIC, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION, and alarms.
  • Zero Broad Storage Scans: Uses Android Photo Picker and SAF rather than broad media-library storage permissions.
  • Foreground Service & Launcher: Persistent notification appears whenever background wake-word or prioritization is active; discovered installed apps stay on-device.

4. Google Workspace & Google Drive (Limited Use)

  • Google Drive (drive.appdata, drive.file): Conversation & memory backups inside your personal Drive.
  • Workspace (Gmail, Calendar, Contacts, Sheets, Forms, Chat): Accessed strictly on explicit request; outbound sends require approval.
Adheres strictly to the Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use). Google user data is never sold, shared for ads, or used to train generalized AI models.

5. Self-Custodial Wallet & AI Controls

  • Local Key Custody: BIP-39 seeds and private keys remain encrypted locally via Android Keystore AES-256-GCM and are never transmitted externally.
  • AI Boundary: Separate Wallet Read & Wallet Write switches in Settings/Wallet govern every agent action behind PIN/Biometric & approval prompts.

6. Storage & Markdown / JSON Portability

Cloud chats and memories live in user-scoped Firebase Firestore records. You can export conversations anytime as Markdown (.md) or JSON (.json).

7. Blockchain RPCs & CoinGecko APIs

Public RPC endpoints and CoinGecko receive only public addresses, signed raw transactions, or coin identifiers when balances/prices are requested.

8. Retention, Deletion, Children & Updates

Delete chats, memories, or local wallets anytime, revoke OAuth in your Google Account, or email srasmarkhor7289@gmail.com for full account deletion. Not intended for children under 13.
