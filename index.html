Aqua AI

Autonomous Android AI Assistant, Workspace Hub & Self-Custodial Multi-Chain Wallet

Overview

Aqua AI (Android package com.aistudio.aquaai.vqrzmn) is a native Android personal AI assistant combining Gemini intelligence, voice conversations, user-controlled live web research, device automation, optional Google Workspace and Google Drive backup tools, and a non-custodial multi-chain cryptocurrency wallet.

Core Capabilities

  • Gemini AI & Live Web Access: Multimodal reasoning, live web search, webpage reading, and public HTTP API inspection governed by your in-app toggles.
  • Google Workspace & Drive (Optional): When explicitly authorized by you, Aqua AI connects to Gmail, Calendar, Contacts, Sheets, Forms, Chat, and Google Drive AppData/Files strictly for user-requested tasks and conversation backups.
  • Self-Custodial Crypto Wallet: Supports Ethereum, BNB Smart Chain, Polygon, Base, Arbitrum, Solana, and Bitcoin using local OS randomness and Android Keystore-backed encrypted key storage. Aqua AI never accesses recovery phrases, private keys, or PINs, and sensitive actions require your explicit approval.
