● GOOGLE OAUTH VERIFICATION • LEAST-PRIVILEGE AUDIT

Permissions Audit — Least-Privilege Google Drive Scopes

Aqua AI enforces Google's strict least-privilege OAuth principle: we request only the minimum scopes required to back up, inspect quota for, and restore conversations and memories that Aqua AI itself creates. Aqua AI intentionally refuses broad Drive scopes (such as full drive or drive.readonly) and never requests Google Workspace scopes so your unrelated personal Drive documents, photos, emails, contacts, and calendars remain completely invisible to the app.

Scope / Permission Scope Identifier What Aqua AI Can Access Why Least-Privilege Matters
App Data Folder
Primary Sync Vault		 https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata (drive.appdata) Only Aqua AI's hidden application-private folder (appDataFolder) storing aqua_ai_history_backup.json. Isolates automated cloud history snapshots from your personal My Drive folders; Aqua AI cannot view or list any other files or folders in your Drive.
App-Created Files Only
Explicit Export & Restore		 https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file (drive.file) Only individual JSON backup files created by Aqua AI (Aqua_AI_History_*.json) or explicitly picked by you, plus Drive /about quota attribution with optional GOOGLE_DRIVE_API_KEY. Prevents blanket read/write access across your Drive; every exported snapshot stays user-owned and deletions require explicit in-app confirmation.
Explicitly Excluded
Never Requested		 drive, drive.readonly, Gmail, Calendar, Contacts, Sheets, Forms, Chat, READ_MEDIA_* Zero access — all Google Workspace repositories, manifests, and tools have been completely removed. Eliminates unnecessary blast radius, protects bystander/workspace privacy, and aligns with Google Play & OAuth API Services User Data Policy.
