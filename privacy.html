1. Information We Access and Process

  • Account Information: When you sign in with Google via Firebase Authentication, Aqua AI receives your Firebase UID, display name, email address, and profile photo URL solely to authenticate your session and isolate your Firestore workspace.
  • Age Tier & Optional Gender Profile: You may provide your age (validated 13+) and an optional gender identity/pronoun preference (Female, Male, Non-binary, Prefer not to say, or Unspecified) in Post-Login Setup or Settings. These values are stored inside your user-scoped Firestore settings document (users/{uid}/settings/preferences) solely to enforce 13+ safety guardrails and adapt conversational tone.
  • User-Initiated Prompts & Attachments: Text messages, voice transcripts, documents, and images selected via Android Photo Picker or Storage Access Framework (SAF) are processed only when you submit them to generate AI responses.
  • Live Web & Network Access: When Full Internet Access is enabled, search queries and public URLs you ask Aqua AI to inspect are retrieved over HTTPS without ad-tech cookies or cross-site tracking SDKs.
  • Session Telemetry (On-Device): Live battery level, charging state, Wi-Fi state, and RAM utilization displayed in the Core/Hub sparkline come from local Android system managers during your active session and are not uploaded as background telemetry profiles.

2. AI Personas, Age Guardrails, Custom Keys & ElevenLabs Voice

  • AI Intelligence Provider (BYOK Supported): Assistant reasoning uses managed Firebase AI Logic by default, or your own optional personal Google AI Studio API key entered during Post-Login Setup or Settings and encrypted on-device via Android Keystore AES-256-GCM.
  • Optional ElevenLabs Voice Studio (Non-Compulsory): If you choose to supply an optional ElevenLabs API key and Voice ID, both are encrypted locally in Android Keystore and only submitted text phrases you speak or preview are sent to ElevenLabs (api.elevenlabs.io) for synthesis. Otherwise Gemini or Android TTS handles voice locally.
  • Personas, Directives & AI Freedom Parameters: Your AI Persona, custom system directive, pinned routines, sampling temperature/Top-P/step limits, and autonomy policy stay saved locally per user in AquaLocalPreferences and govern only prompts you submit. In Teen Mode (ages 13–17), autonomy is capped at Balanced and safety guardrails are injected into the prompt context.

3. Android Permissions & Foreground Service

  • Least-Privilege Runtime Permissions: Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO for push-to-talk, Gemini Live, or optional wake-word listening), Camera (QR scanning and visual capture), Biometrics (local wallet unlock/signing), Coarse Location (opt-in local map context), and Alarms (only on explicit user request).
  • Zero Broad Storage Scanning: Aqua AI uses Android's zero-permission Photo Picker and Storage Access Framework (OpenDocument / CreateDocument) rather than broad media-library storage permissions.
  • Foreground Priority Service Transparency: When you enable background wake-word listening or background task prioritization, Android runs a visible persistent notification (AquaWakeWordService, type microphone|dataSync) with one-tap pause and stop controls.
  • No Home Launcher & Local Package Lookup: Aqua AI no longer ships a custom Android Home launcher or live wallpaper service. Standard Android PackageManager queries used solely by the in-app Command Palette or explicit AI app-launch commands remain strictly on-device and are never synced to Firebase.

4. Google Workspace & Google Drive Data (Limited Use Disclosure)

Aqua AI requests Google Workspace or Google Drive OAuth permissions strictly when you explicitly connect those integrations:

  • Google Drive (drive.appdata, drive.file): Used exclusively to back up and restore your Aqua AI conversation history and memories into your own Google Drive account.
  • Google Workspace (Gmail, Calendar, Contacts, Sheets, Forms, Chat): Accessed solely to fulfill explicit commands (reading, summarizing, drafting, scheduling, or sending with your approval). Outbound sends always display an explicit confirmation modal first.
Aqua AI's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres strictly to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is never sold, shared with data brokers, used for advertising, or used to train generalized AI/ML models.

5. Self-Custodial Crypto Wallet & AI Boundary Controls

  • Local Key Custody: BIP-39 recovery phrases and private keys are created on-device via OS SecureRandom and encrypted inside private device storage backed by Android Keystore AES-256-GCM. Mnemonics, raw keys, and PINs are never transmitted to Firebase, Gemini models, or external servers.
  • User-Governed AI Boundary: You separately control Wallet Read Permission (inspecting public addresses, balances, allowances, and transactions) and Wallet Write Permission (preparing transfers, revocations, or vault creation) in Settings and Wallet. Signing always requires your explicit approval plus PIN/Biometric authentication when enabled.

6. Data Storage, Security & Markdown / JSON Portability

Cloud conversations, skills, routines, and memories reside inside user-scoped Firebase Firestore collections protected by zero-trust authentication rules so only your authenticated account can read or write them. Local preferences, wallet vaults, and offline caches remain private on-device. You can export active conversations anytime to clean Markdown (.md) or JSON (.json), or export a password-encrypted portable wallet JSON independent of Firebase.

7. Third-Party Blockchain & Market Services

When refreshing balances, estimating gas, resolving ENS names, querying CoinGecko token prices, or broadcasting already-signed transactions, Aqua AI sends only public wallet addresses, signed raw payloads, or asset IDs directly to public RPC endpoints and CoinGecko APIs.

8. Retention, Data Deletion, Children's Privacy & Contact

  • Deletion & Revocation: You can delete conversations, memories, skills, routines, or local wallets directly inside Aqua AI, reset or change your Age/Gender profile inside Settings, revoke Google OAuth permissions in your Google Account Security panel, or request complete cloud account and data deletion by emailing srasmarkhor7289@gmail.com.
  • Children's Privacy (13+ Gate & Teen Mode): Aqua AI is strictly restricted to users aged 13 and older (or the minimum digital consent age in your jurisdiction). If an age below 13 is entered, on-device policy blocks chat and live voice execution immediately. Users aged 13–17 operate under automatic Teen Mode guardrails.
  • Policy Updates: Material updates will be reflected by an updated Effective Date and version summary on this portal.
