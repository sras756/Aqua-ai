1. Information We Access & Portal Sign-In

  • App Account Authentication: When you sign in with Google via Firebase Authentication, Aqua AI receives your Firebase UID, display name, email address, and profile photo URL solely to authenticate your session and isolate your user data.
  • Website Sign in with Google (Privacy & Terms Portal): When you sign in on this web portal via Google Identity Services (accounts.google.com/gsi/client), your signed Google ID token (JWT) is decoded strictly client-side in your browser to display your name, email, and avatar and store a local policy acknowledgement receipt inside browser localStorage. No third-party tracking server stores your website session.
  • Age Tier & Optional Gender Profile: You may provide your age (validated 13+) and an optional gender identity/pronoun preference stored inside your user-scoped Firestore settings document (users/{uid}/settings/preferences) solely to enforce 13+ safety guardrails and adapt conversational tone.
  • User-Initiated Prompts & Attachments: Text messages, voice transcripts, documents, and images selected via Android Photo Picker or Storage Access Framework (SAF) are processed only when you submit them to generate AI responses.

2. AI Personas, Age Guardrails, Custom Keys & ElevenLabs Voice

  • AI Intelligence Provider (BYOK Supported): Assistant reasoning uses managed Firebase AI Logic by default, or your own optional personal Google AI Studio API key encrypted on-device via Android Keystore AES-256-GCM.
  • Optional ElevenLabs Voice Studio (Non-Compulsory): If you choose to supply an optional ElevenLabs API key and Voice ID, both are encrypted locally in Android Keystore and only submitted phrases you speak or preview are sent to ElevenLabs (api.elevenlabs.io).
  • Personas, Directives & AI Freedom Parameters: Your AI Persona, custom directive, pinned routines, and AI Freedom sliders stay saved locally per user in AquaLocalPreferences. In Teen Mode (ages 13–17), autonomy is capped at Balanced automatically.

3. Android Permissions & Foreground Service Transparency

  • Least-Privilege Runtime Permissions: Opt-in Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO), Camera (QR scanning and vision), Biometrics (wallet unlock), Coarse Location (opt-in map context), and Alarms. Legacy contact and calendar permissions (READ_CONTACTS, READ_CALENDAR) have been permanently removed.
  • Zero Broad Storage Scans: Uses Android Photo Picker and SAF (OpenDocument / CreateDocument) rather than broad media storage permissions.
  • On-Device Battery & Memory Diagnostics: Device battery and heap memory telemetry are inspected strictly on-device for live chat sparklines and never sold or used for tracking.
  • Foreground Priority Service Transparency: Optional background wake-word/automation mode shows a persistent Android notification (AquaWakeWordService, type microphone|dataSync) with pause and stop controls.

4. Google Drive Vault & Approved API Key (Limited Use Disclosure)

Aqua AI removes all broad mail, calendar, sheets, and personal contacts scopes, requesting strictly least-privilege Google Drive OAuth permissions upon explicit action:

  • Google Drive Only (drive.appdata, drive.file): Used exclusively to check your Drive quota, list Aqua backups, export/restore JSON snapshots of your conversation history and memories, and delete backups upon explicit modal approval.
  • Approved Google Drive API Key: Supports approved project attribution via GOOGLE_DRIVE_API_KEY (via Secrets / BuildConfig or per-user encrypted Keystore override) alongside your OAuth 2.0 Bearer token.
Aqua AI's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres strictly to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google Drive data is never sold, shared with data brokers, used for advertising, or used to train generalized AI/ML models.

5. Self-Custodial Crypto Wallet & AI Boundary Controls

  • Local Key Custody: BIP-39 recovery phrases and private keys are created on-device via OS SecureRandom and encrypted inside private device storage backed by Android Keystore AES-256-GCM. Mnemonics, raw keys, and PINs are never transmitted to Firebase, Gemini models, or external servers.
  • User-Governed AI Boundary: Separate Wallet Read Permission and Wallet Write Permission controls govern AI access. Signing always requires your explicit approval plus PIN/Biometric authentication when enabled.

6. Data Storage, Security & Markdown / JSON Portability

Cloud conversations, skills, routines, and memories reside inside user-scoped Firebase Firestore collections protected by zero-trust authentication rules so only your authenticated account can read or write them. You can export active conversations anytime to Markdown (.md) or JSON (.json), export device diagnostic summaries, or create a password-encrypted portable wallet JSON.

7. Third-Party Blockchain & Market Services

When refreshing balances, estimating gas, resolving ENS names, querying CoinGecko token prices, or broadcasting signed transactions, Aqua AI sends only public wallet addresses, signed raw payloads, or asset IDs directly to public RPC endpoints and CoinGecko APIs.

8. Retention, Data Deletion, Children's Privacy & Contact

  • Deletion & Revocation: Delete conversations, memories, skills, routines, Drive backups, or local wallets directly inside Aqua AI, clear browser localStorage / Google portal sign-in on this site, revoke OAuth in your Google Account Security panel, or request complete cloud account deletion at srasmarkhor7289@gmail.com.
  • Children's Privacy (13+ Gate & Teen Mode): Aqua AI is strictly restricted to users aged 13 and older. If an age below 13 is entered, on-device policy blocks chat and live voice execution immediately. Users aged 13–17 operate under automatic Teen Mode guardrails.
  • Policy Updates: Material updates will be reflected by an updated Effective Date and version summary on this portal.
