Aqua AI

Autonomous Android AI Assistant, Workspace Hub & Self-Custodial Multi-Chain Wallet

Overview

Aqua AI (Android package com.aistudio.aquaai.vqrzmn) is a native Android personal AI assistant combining Gemini intelligence, voice conversations, live web research, device automation, optional Google Workspace and Google Drive backup tools, and a self-custodial multi-chain cryptocurrency wallet.

Core Capabilities

  • Gemini AI & Live Web Access: Multimodal reasoning, structured research, and live web/API grounding controlled directly by the user.
  • Google Workspace & Drive (Optional): When explicitly authorized by the user, Aqua AI connects to Gmail, Calendar, Contacts, Sheets, Forms, Chat, and Google Drive AppData/Files strictly for user-requested actions and history backups.
  • Self-Custodial Crypto Wallet: Supports Ethereum, BNB Smart Chain, Polygon, Base, Arbitrum, Solana, and Bitcoin with local OS randomness and Android Keystore-backed encrypted key storage.
