Privacy Policy for Aqua AI

Effective Date: November 14, 2026  |  App Package: com.aistudio.aquaai.vqrzmn

Developer Contact: srasmarkhor7289@gmail.com

1. Information We Access and Process

  • Account Information: When you sign in with Google via Firebase Authentication, Aqua AI receives your Firebase user ID, display name, email address, and profile photo URL to sync your preferences, memories, and chat sessions.
  • AI Prompts & Attachments: Messages, voice transcripts, and files you submit to the assistant are sent to Firebase AI Logic (Gemini) to generate responses.
  • Device Permissions (Opt-In): Camera (QR scanning and photo capture), Microphone (voice commands and optional wake-word service), Biometrics (local wallet unlock/transaction confirmation), Approximate Location (optional local context), and Alarms (when requested).

2. Google Workspace & Google Drive Data (Limited Use Disclosure)

Aqua AI only requests Google Drive or Google Workspace OAuth scopes when you explicitly connect those integrations:

  • Google Drive (drive.appdata, drive.file): Used solely to back up and restore your Aqua AI conversation history and memory snapshots to your own Google Drive account.
  • Google Workspace (Gmail, Calendar, Contacts, Sheets, Forms, Chat): Accessed solely on your explicit request to read, compose, schedule, or inspect items inside your account.

Aqua AI's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is never sold, used for advertising, or used to train generalized AI/ML models.

3. Self-Custodial Crypto Wallet Keys

Recovery phrases (mnemonics) and private keys for Aqua Wallet are generated on-device using operating-system secure randomness and stored locally using Android Keystore-backed encryption. Private keys and recovery phrases are never transmitted to Firebase, AI models, analytics services, or third-party servers.

4. Data Storage, Retention & Deletion

Cloud conversations and memories are stored in your user-scoped Firebase Firestore records. You may sign out, clear conversations, revoke Google OAuth permissions in your Google Account settings, or request account data deletion by emailing srasmarkhor7289@gmail.com.
